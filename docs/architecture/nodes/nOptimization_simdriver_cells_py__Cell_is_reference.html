The natural baseline: no split, no zoning, the legacy scheduler. This predicate was written out twice, verbatim, in `scenario.py` and `run_simulation.py` — and those two MUST agree, because one picks the cell the driver treats as the baseline and the other writes the reference name into `run_layout.json` for every downstream what-if to diff against. Two copies of a predicate that must agree is a bug with a delay on it.
