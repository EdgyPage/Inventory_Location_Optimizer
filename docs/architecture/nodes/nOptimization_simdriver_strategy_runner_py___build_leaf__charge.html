def _charge(section: str, seconds: float) -> None
Charge a span measured OUTSIDE this leaf to one of its sections. Coupled units only: the driver times the site drive once and hands every leaf the same number, because the drive is the SITE's receive and put drain done on every leaf's behalf. Charged rather than lapped so that no leaf's lap is ever open while another leaf, or the site, is running -- which is the whole discipline that keeps a leaf's sections a partition of that leaf's own work.
