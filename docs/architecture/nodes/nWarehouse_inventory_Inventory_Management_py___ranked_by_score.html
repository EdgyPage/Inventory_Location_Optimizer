function _ranked_by_score

layer wh_inventory · file Warehouse/inventory/Inventory_Management.py

def _ranked_by_score(taken: list, prefers_low: bool) -> list
Attach each placement's 0-based rank within its group, best first. `prefers_low` comes from the pool, so rank 0 always means "the best choice available" rather than "the smallest number" -- three arms (tmax, rank_maxlabor, expn) optimise upward on purpose, and a consumer should not have to know which. Unscored placements (a policy with nothing to report for that unit, or a `None` bin) rank NULL rather than last: they were not worse, they were not measured. Equal scores share the LOWEST rank, competition-style, so a tie never implies an ordering the policy did not make -- the tie was broken by candidate order, not by the objective.
