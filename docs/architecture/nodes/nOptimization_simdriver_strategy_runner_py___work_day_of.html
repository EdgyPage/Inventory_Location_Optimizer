The working day a payload describes, as a `WorkDay`. ONE expression, two callers: a leaf builds its release schedule from it and the site put pool divides it. Two constructions of the same day would be two days the moment one of them picked up a fallback the other did not, and the symptom would be a put crew whose whistle was minutes away from its own pickers'.
