Paired per-element improvement percentages with the UNDEFINED pairs dropped. THE way to build a sample of paired improvements: every statistic downstream (a mean, a median, a confidence interval, a test) is only honest over the pairs where the comparison exists. Returns a float array that may be shorter than its inputs — and empty when no pair is comparable.
