Optimization.strategies
strategies.py — data-driven registry of placement (assignment) strategies. Each Strategy names an assignment policy and knows how to wire it onto an Inventory_Manager for the reorder phase. The whole comparison pipeline (run_simulation orchestration, strategy_runner worker, run_analysis/Comparison_Plots labels via sim_meta) iterates this list, so adding a strategy is one entry here. Strategies share the same uniform INITIAL stocking by default (done in the worker before build() is called) and differ in how reorder waves are placed. A strategy may override stocking with a `stock` hook (e.g. optimal_reslot stocks at the pure-global-W optimum) and may enable bounded per-batch re-slotting via `reslot_frac`.
