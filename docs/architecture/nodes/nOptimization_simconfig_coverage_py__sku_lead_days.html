def sku_lead_days(order, transit_days: float=0.0, lead_unit_days: float=1.0) -> float
`lead_days_s = lead_time_mean_s · lead_unit_days + transit_days`: the SKU's expected order-to-shelf lead in days -- its supplier lead (authored in batches, one batch a day under the era) plus the pair's day-grid transit ("Declare the coverage against the inbound lead", decisions 2 and 4). Never negative; 0 on a lead-free catalogue with no pipeline, which is every catalogue the generator has authored so far.
