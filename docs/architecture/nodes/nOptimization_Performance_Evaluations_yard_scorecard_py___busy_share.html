def _busy_share(bdf, crew: int, S: float, n_days: int) -> str
One frame's receiver-busy share against the SITE crew and the SITE's work days. Factored out of `_receiver_busy` so the site number and the per-channel shares are computed by ONE function against ONE denominator. That is the whole point of printing them together: a share is of a whole the channel does not own (ADR-0005 says so in as many words), so it is only readable beside the total it is a share OF.
