@dataclass(frozen=True)
class PickerProfile
A pool of one kind of picker: a name, its pick-time cost regression (a PickConfig carrying coefficients + travel speeds), how many work concurrently, and what KIND of actor they are. `mode` is not a new idea here -- the two pools have been called `store_machine` and `fulfillment_walker` for as long as they have existed, and `simconfig/constants.py` comments them as "machine order-picker pool" and "human-walker pool". Nothing read either name. The distinction was real, written down in prose, and inert; now it is a value, and `crew()` turns this pool into the domain's own actor objects. Both fields default to today's values (a picking crew on foot), so a construction that does not care -- there are several in tests and Diagnostics -- is unchanged.
