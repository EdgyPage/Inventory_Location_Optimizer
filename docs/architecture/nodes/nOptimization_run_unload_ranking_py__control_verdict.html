def control_verdict(ranked: list, reference: str) -> dict
What the RIDER says, read as a control rather than a replication. Under FIFO restock a placement-gain policy degenerates to arrival order (memory `pick-owed-s-replaces-flow-totals-for-unload-ranking`), so the rider's cells that read BYTE-IDENTICAL to the reference on score and overage are the policies that did nothing at all under it -- a fact about the pairing, reported as one -- and the cells that moved are the policies that reorder on a rule independent of placement. Neither is a ranking of unloading policies, which is why this never vetoes the winner pair's order.
