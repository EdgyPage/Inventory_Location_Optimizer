def line_floor(line, floor_lines: float) -> int
`L_s = ceil(floor_lines × E[q_s])`, at least 1: the least stock a SKU ever holds, in units, read off its stamped line law. `floor_lines` is the declared number of lines (1.0 by default: one pick's worth); the mean is rounded UP so the shelf is never below the expected line. Refuses a non-positive `floor_lines` -- a zero would be the unit floor by the back door.
