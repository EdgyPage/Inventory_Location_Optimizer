class InstrumentError(RuntimeError)
A nonzero `released_late` on a DRAINED day: the instrument contradicts itself. A drained day means nothing was cut and nothing stood at close-out, and release waits on the picker clock, so a batch released late into such a day is impossible unless the ledger or the release clamp is wrong. Raised, never returned as a failed clause: a failed clause says the SITE is out of equilibrium; this says the MEASUREMENT is.
