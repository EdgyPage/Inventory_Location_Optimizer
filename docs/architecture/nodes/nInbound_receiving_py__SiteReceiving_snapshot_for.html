def snapshot_for(self, leaf) -> tuple
`(depth, unloaded, cut, seconds)` — THIS leaf's share of the site dock. The site-scoped replacement for `Inventory_Manager.receiving_snapshot`, which refuses on a coupled leaf because all four counters are the site's and three of them RESET: the first caller would take the site's whole batch and leave the other channel reporting an idle dock. Here the reset happens once, in `_partition`, and each leaf is served its own share.
