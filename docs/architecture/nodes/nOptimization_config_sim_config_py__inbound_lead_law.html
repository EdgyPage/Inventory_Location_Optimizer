def inbound_lead_law() -> dict | None
The trailer pipeline's LEAD LAW alone -- `{'trailer_type', 'lead_s', 'lead_sigma'}`, or **None** when no trailer type is named (the pipeline is off, structurally). The three keys `inbound_spec()` reads for the lead, factored out because the coverage record needs them BEFORE the receiving crew exists: `era_coverage.lead_block` derives the day-grid transit at setup (`coverage.transit_day_law`), and `inbound_spec()`'s "a yard nobody can unload" guard would refuse that read under the era, where the crew is derived only after the fixed point has declared the levels. No guard here: this accessor answers what the lead IS; `inbound_spec()` still refuses every contradiction the moment the worker payload is built. Reads CONFIG at call time; the median is converted from minutes to seconds here and nowhere else (the same conversion `inbound_spec()` records as `lead_s`).
