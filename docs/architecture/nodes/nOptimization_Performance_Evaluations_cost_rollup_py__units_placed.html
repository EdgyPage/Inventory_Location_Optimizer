def units_placed(ctx) -> dict
{(cell, pair, config, channel): units put away per wave} from the per-run tables. The denominator that turns seconds into an engineering number. Read from the leaf summaries rather than assumed, and averaged across arms within a leaf: every arm faces the same demand, so they agree to rounding, and taking the mean makes a missing arm a small error rather than a wrong one.
