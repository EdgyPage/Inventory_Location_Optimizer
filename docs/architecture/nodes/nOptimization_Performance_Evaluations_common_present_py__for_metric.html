def for_metric(name: str, samples=None) -> tuple
(conv, axis_label) for one metric name against the samples that share its axis. Pool EVERY series that will land on the axis into `samples`: a duration's unit is resolved from its own magnitude, and resolving it per-series is how two boxes on one panel end up in different units with one label between them. `name` may be either the per-batch key or the historical aggregate spelling — `production_time` and `productivity_hours` are the same quantity and resolve to the same declaration.
