def _cluster_map_choose_aisle(by_aisle, prefs_by_aisle, row, aisle_idx_sets, freq_by_idx, target)
Cohesion-first aisle: max Σ(lift−1)·f to members, tie-break / cold-start by anchor gap. Same argmax as ``max(live, key=(lift, -anchor_gap))`` but LAZY: the O(B) anchor-gap scan is replaced by an O(log) ``_closest_abs`` bisect on the aisle's pre-sorted ``prefs_by_aisle`` and is evaluated ONLY for the aisles tied at the max lift (in the warm case, one aisle wins on lift → no gap work at all). ``row`` is the SKU's pre-sliced affinity row (hoisted once per unit).
