Optimization.Performance_Evaluations.comparison
Shared over-time metric specs for the comparison graphs. The same five trajectories drive faceted/, overlay/, and top/ (per-config) and the cross-profile aggregate suite. `agg=True` appends the '(× baseline)' unit used when the curves are baseline-normalized ratios rather than raw values.
