function _rank_channel

layer optimization · file Optimization/run_restock_selection.py

def _rank_channel(leaves, log) -> dict
Rank every restock rule in one channel. Lower hours win. An arm's score is its `ss_prod_total` SUMMED over the channel's (profile, config) leaves — a steady-state per-batch mean of production seconds, summed, then reported in hours. Every arm is measured on the identical leaf set, so the sum is like-for-like; a MIN over leaves would let two rules be scored on different profiles and is exactly the "ranks are not additive" mistake one level down. A RULE's score is the minimum over its own arms, because selecting a rule takes both of them and what makes the rule worth carrying into phase 2 is that one of its arms performs. Both arm totals are recorded, so a reader can see when the two disagree. An arm missing from a leaf, or reading non-finite there, disqualifies its RULE: a partial sum would silently rank a rule that was measured on fewer profiles than its rivals as cheaper than all of them.
