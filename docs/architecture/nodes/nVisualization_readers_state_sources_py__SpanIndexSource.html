class SpanIndexSource(StateSource)
The frame from the sidecar's log-built span index. None = no such index. The span gives the bin's SKU and the qty it was filled with at `t_from`; the qty NOW is that minus the picks since. A keyframe at or below `batch` is used as the qty anchor whenever one exists -- not for correctness, but to bound the pick scan to one keyframe interval instead of the whole run. Without keyframes the answer is the same, read from a longer range.
