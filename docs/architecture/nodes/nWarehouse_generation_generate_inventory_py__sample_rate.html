def sample_rate(spec: dict, rng: random.Random) -> float
Sample a demand rate (pick frequency or quantity-per-pick) from *spec*. Returns a float; the caller (Order.build) clamps frequency to (0,1] and qty_rate to the integer range [MIN_QTY, MAX_QTY]. Supported: uniform low, high fixed value normal mean, std mixture components: [{prob, spec}, ...]
