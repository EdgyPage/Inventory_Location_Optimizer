def _execute_topup(order, bin_, n, *, source=None, queue=None)
ADR-0003's own-bin rung: a PLACE into a bin that was already occupied. Shares `_place_seq` with `_execute_placement` deliberately -- both are places, the sequence is what orders them within a batch, and two counters would make `(run_id, batch_id, seq)` collide on the primary key. `score` stays NULL: no policy chose this bin, the SKU's own on-hand did, and a zero would claim a placement was scored perfectly when it was not scored at all.
