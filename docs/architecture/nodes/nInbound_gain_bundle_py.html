Inbound.gain_bundle
gain_bundle — which arm owns a unit, and what the evaluator may ask of it. One mixed trailer is priced per unit by the OWNING channel's arm, so something has to answer "whose arm is this?" before anything can be priced. These three do, through one protocol: - `GainBundle` — everything arm-specific the evaluator needs, driver-built and driver-injected, because `Inbound -> wh_inventory / wh_placement` are forbidden edges and the broker holds what it is handed. - `OneOwnerBundle` — a single-channel run: `for_key` ignores the key and returns the one bundle it holds. - `SiteGainBundle` — two arms over one mixed trailer, resolved by the key's own regime. ## Why this is its own module The deletion test again, and `gain.py:57-60` states it: deleting the trio brings `if coupled:` back into the PRICING PATH, which is exactly what it exists to prevent. There is one resolution rule and no coupling branch anywhere below it.
