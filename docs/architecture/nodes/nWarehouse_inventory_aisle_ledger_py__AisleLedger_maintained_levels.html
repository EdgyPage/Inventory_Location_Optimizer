def maintained_levels(self) -> tuple[str, ...]
The priced levels this ledger is entitled to be reconciled on. For a policy's ledger these are the levels its family was handed and therefore maintains -- `demand_sum` for every family, plus `pick_load_sum` on the two labour balancers and `vol_sum` on `rank_cartlabor`. Derived from the wiring, so it cannot disagree with it; hand-listing the same fact a second time is what ticket 03 calls `ledger_terms` and what this makes unnecessary.
