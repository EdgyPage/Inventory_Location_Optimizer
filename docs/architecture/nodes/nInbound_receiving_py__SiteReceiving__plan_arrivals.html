STEP 1: PLANS-AT-ARRIVAL -- every trailer that joined the yard is packed NOW. Stamps the units immediately, so a pallet that stands three batches in the yard is three batches old when it finally reaches floor space. The merchandise stays in `_deferred_qty`: nothing is queued until a crew actually pulls it.
