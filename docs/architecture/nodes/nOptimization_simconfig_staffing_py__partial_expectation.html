def partial_expectation(mean: float, sd: float, cap: float) -> float
`E[(W - cap)^+]` for `W ~ Normal(mean, sd)`: `sd·[φ(z) - z·(1 - Φ(z))]` at `z = (cap - mean) / sd` -- the expected overflow of a day's work past the crew's cap. Degenerates to `max(0, mean - cap)` at `sd = 0`.
