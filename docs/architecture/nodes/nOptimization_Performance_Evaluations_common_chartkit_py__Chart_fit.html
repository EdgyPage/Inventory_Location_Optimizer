def fit(self)
Grow the canvas until nothing is clipped, keeping the footer band clear. Called automatically by `save`. The reserved bands solve the collisions chartkit can predict, but not the one it cannot: how wide a caller's own tick labels turn out to be. Seventeen rotated 20-character arm names need more left and bottom margin than any fixed constant should promise, and the old answer — crop with a tight bbox — is what cut the provenance line off in the first place. So measure after drawing, and EXPAND rather than crop: the panels keep the size they were designed at, and the overflow gets canvas of its own.
