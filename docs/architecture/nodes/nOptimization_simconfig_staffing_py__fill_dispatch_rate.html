def fill_dispatch_rate(fill: dict, *, doors: int, door_team: int | None, day_seconds: float) -> dict
Units per site day the fill dispatches, and whether the doors bind the drain. The DECLARED rule (inbound-throughput Q15/Q20): arrivals press the drain at `ratio` of its rate. The drain rate is what the SEATED crew can unload: a door team of `cap` at each of `doors` doors seats at most `doors x cap` receivers however many the fill derived, so a fill crew the dock cannot seat drains at the seats' rate -- and a dispatch priced off the whole crew would press it past 1 and never be a queue at all. Returns `{units_per_day, seated, seat_bound, dispatch_days}`.
