def _leaf_is_complete(run_dir: str) -> bool
Today's completeness test for ONE channel-run dir — no new marker. The marker present AND the resume file absent, which is the pair of facts `supervisor._finalize_config_run` writes in that order (site-dock 16 closed the window where a kill could leave neither). A coupled unit needs nothing recorded beyond this: finalize runs only when every member uid succeeded, so TWO finalized leaves cannot exist without a successful unit, and the pair of markers already carries the fact a third one would record. A third marker could also tear in its own right -- a kill after both leaves finalize but before it is written re-runs a FINISHED pair -- and adding a third thing to keep in sync is not how two things being out of sync gets fixed. ONE function, and the uncoupled skip guard reads it too: the coupled rule is this same test applied to two directories instead of one, and two spellings of "complete" would be two things to keep in step over exactly the question this file is reconciling.
