class SimBooks
The sim driver's per-cell books behind `WorkPool`'s callbacks. Per cell, in the shape the old `_run_pool` kept: `meta[group_key] = {sim_skeleton, members}`, `done` uids, `finalized` group keys. Per cell because uids and group keys repeat across cells. `on_success` absorbs a unit and finalizes every group it completes; `on_failure` is the `strategy FAILED` line; `sweep` is the safety pass the old `_supervise` ran at the end (a group whose last member landed but whose finalize raised gets one more chance).
