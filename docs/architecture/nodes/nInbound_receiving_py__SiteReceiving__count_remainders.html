What the whistle cost: the remainders standing on STAGED trailers, counted once. Returns `left` in storage units. Computed above the whistle test, not inside it: a drain that ran out of WORK leaves the same remainder as one that ran out of DAY, and only one of those is a cut -- the level says what was standing either way.
