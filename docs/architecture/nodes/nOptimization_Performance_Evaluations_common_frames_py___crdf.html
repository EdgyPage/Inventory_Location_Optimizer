def _crdf(rows)
The RAW carryover rows, one per (batch, reason, sku) -- the equilibrium check's flows. Deliberately unfolded: `equilibrium.demand_flows` needs the per-SKU rows to count a re-attempted supply failure once and to recover fresh demand from the previous batch's whole carry, and it selects reasons itself (`sim_semantics`' ByDiscriminator on `reason` is why no total is taken here). Empty with its columns on a vintage without the table, which the check reports as unrecorded rather than as everything served.
