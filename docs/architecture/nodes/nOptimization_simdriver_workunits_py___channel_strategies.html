def _channel_strategies(ch) -> list
The arms one channel runs: its restock subset, less the uniform-stock arms in a FILL TRIAL. A fill has no initial stock, so the one thing that mode governs does not exist, and the worker refuses it (`_build_arm`); dropping them HERE keeps a fill matrix from planning units it knows will fail. One helper, because the arm list is read twice -- by the prepare and by the coupled-pair reconciler -- and two spellings could disagree about which arms a pair holds.
