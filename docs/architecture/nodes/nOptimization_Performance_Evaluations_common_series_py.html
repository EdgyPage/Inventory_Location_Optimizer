Optimization.Performance_Evaluations.common.series
Per-strategy time-series construction, the series.json dump, top-N selection, and the cross-profile aggregation (verbatim from the retired Comparison_Plots). `_build_series` is wrapped by EvalContext.series(); `_aggregate_series` by AggregateContext.agg_series(). `_dump_series` and `_select_top` are used by the config.series graph and the top_metric graph respectively.
