percent view — each arm's labor gain against its throughput gain, vs the baseline. The question two independent reviewers asked and no artifact answered: WHICH ONE DO WE ADOPT. Every other headline figure ranks on one quantity, and the podium is not the same on both — an arm can save labor by spreading work to the least-loaded aisle and finish the day later for it. Here the baseline is the origin, the shaded corner is "better on both", and an arm in an off-diagonal quadrant is trading one for the other in the direction its position names.
