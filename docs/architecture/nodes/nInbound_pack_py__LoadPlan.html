class LoadPlan
One inbound delivery: what arrived, and the storage units it became. Immutable in the sense that matters -- the units are packed once at receipt and the plan is the record of that. `whole_qty` is the quantity this delivery WOULD have been had the shipment not been split, and it is what makes `unsplit()` answerable; it equals `received` for a delivery that was not split.
