def shift_end(cap_end_s: float, last_finish_s: float, drained: bool) -> float
When the drain-or-cap shift ENDED: the drain instant or the cap, whichever first. `drained` is the CALLER's judgment — no standing work remains (released-but-unpicked demand, queued puts and held items, the dock floor; never merchandise in transit) and nothing was cut at the whistle. This function owns only the arithmetic: a drain that happened before the cap ends the shift there ("off the clock"); anything else — work standing, or a finish past the whistle (START-gate overtime) — ends it at the cap, because the cap came first. Days stay origin-aligned either way: an early end stops the labour, never the calendar.
