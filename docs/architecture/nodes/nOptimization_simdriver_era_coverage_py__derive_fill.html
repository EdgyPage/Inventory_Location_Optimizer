def derive_fill(req: dict, extra: dict, min_headroom: float) -> dict
The bins each bucket must HOLD under the era, and the fill that implies: hold_b = max(requirement_b + E[extra_b], requirement_b / (1 - min_headroom)) fill_b = requirement_b / hold_b (1 - min_headroom on an empty bucket) -- "Band the own-bin share and the free-index depth", decision 7: the warehouse holds its declaration AND the stationary fragmentation base stock creates (`fragmentation.section_fragmentation`, the `expected_extra` per bucket), floored at a declared minimum headroom for what the chain does not model (settings `MIN_HEADROOM`). `expected_extra` can be NEGATIVE (a remainder unit migrating down a tier), and a bucket can carry extra with no requirement at all (the tier it migrates INTO); both read correctly here, the first taking the headroom floor, the second sized to its extra. Pure: `{bucket: {'requirement', 'expected_extra', 'hold', 'fill', 'headroom_floored'}}` over the union of both maps' buckets, `headroom_floored` saying the minimum bound (the fragmentation alone would have left less free). Refuses a headroom outside [0, 1).
