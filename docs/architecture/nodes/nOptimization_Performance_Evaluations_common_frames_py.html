Optimization.Performance_Evaluations.common.frames
DataFrame builders + steady-state series alignment (verbatim from the monoliths). `_bdf`/`_tdf` turn the namedtuple stat lists from Picking_Data into the wide per-batch / per-task frames every graph consumes; `_roll` is the rolling-mean helper; `_metric_series` and `_aligned` build the paired matrices the stats suite tests.
