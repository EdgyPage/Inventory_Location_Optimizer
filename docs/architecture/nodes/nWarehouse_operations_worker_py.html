Warehouse.operations.worker
worker — one actor, and a pool of them, with the two id spaces kept apart. ## The two id spaces, and why there are two Every existing reader assumes picker ids are **dense and per-crew**: `assign_tasks` partitions work into `num_pickers` buckets and the bucket index IS the id; `Simulation_Analytics._group_events_by_picker` allocates exactly `k_pickers` lists and now RAISES on anything outside `[0, k)`; `_ProgressAPIMixin.progress_at` enumerates `range(num_pickers)`; and the `picker_events` table and the viewer are built on the same assumption. A second crew breaks that the moment it shares an event stream — its actors carry ids from a different space, and the old `0 <= pid < k_pickers` filter used to swallow them silently. So a `Worker` carries both: `local_id` dense within its own crew, `0..size-1`. This is what goes to `picker_events` and to everything that predates a second crew. It is NOT unique across crews. `uid` unique across every crew in the run. This is what goes to the merged event stream, beside a role and a mode, so a row can say WHO without ambiguity. Keeping them as two named fields rather than one clever encoding is the point: a `uid` that leaked into a `local_id` slot would land inside `[0, k)` and be accepted.
