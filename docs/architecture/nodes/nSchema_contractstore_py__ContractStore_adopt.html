def adopt(self, doc: dict, *, label: str='', source_fp: str | None=None, commit: str | None=None, created: str | None=None) -> str
Store `doc`, make it the head, record its provenance. Returns its id. Idempotent: adopting the current head refreshes the mutable `source_fingerprint` and nothing else. The previous head becomes the new entry's `parent`, which is what restores the ordering that hashes inherently lack.
