class _Leaf
One channel leaf of a work unit: what it is, and its two halves as callables. `step(i)` runs batch `i`; `finish()` does the run-end flush and returns the leaf's result dict. Both close over `_build_leaf`'s setup, so the leaf's manager, crews, clocks and accumulators are reachable only through them -- which is the point: a driver sequences leaves and does not reach into one. A site-scoped coordinator (site-dock 01/04) is built ABOVE the leaves and injected through their payloads, never fished out of one here.
