def implied_coverage(orders, lines_per_day: float) -> dict
What the section's CURRENT stock levels are worth in days at `lines_per_day` -- the catalogue's implicit coverage, recorded before the first rescaling so the record can say what the generation-batch denomination amounted to on this pair. Demand-weighted mean and the plain median of `Q_s / d_s`; `sum_q` for the size before/after.
