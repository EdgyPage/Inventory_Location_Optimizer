def _window_arg(text: str | None) -> tuple[int, int] | None
`LO-HI` as a pair of working days, or the literal `campaign` for the funnel's own measured window -- which is DECLARED, in `whatif_config.CAMPAIGN_WINDOW_DAYS`, and read here rather than retyped ("Re-size the funnel in site days"). A campaign leaf judged against a hand-typed window is a utilization reported against a warm-up the constants never saw, and nothing in the output would say so. The days are SITE days: under the era one batch is one 28,800 s site day, three times shorter than the calendar day the yard fee accrues in.
