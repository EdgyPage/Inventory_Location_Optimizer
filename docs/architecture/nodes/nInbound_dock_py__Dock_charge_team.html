def charge_team(self, idxs, dur: float)
Book `dur` to the team member free earliest; return (start, worker index). Does NOT accrue `seconds`, unlike `charge` — deliberately. The standing drain charges teams in clock order but accrues `seconds` in the CANONICAL handoff order, so the batch's float sum associates identically whatever the allocation mode — a sum accrued in charge order differs from the merged run's by one ulp, which is exactly the kind of "identical except labor" leak the containment property forbids. The caller that charges a team also hands off, and accrues there, once per record.
