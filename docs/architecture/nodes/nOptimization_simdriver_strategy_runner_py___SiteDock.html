class _SiteDock
The SITE's inbound, at UNIT scope: one dock, one yard, one coordinator, one DB. The receiving twin of `_build_put_pool`'s `PutawayPool`, and built for the same three reasons: the crew is the pair's derived receiving crew (ONE site total, which the leaves no longer carry), the uid block chains off the put pool's so a receiver is the same person in both DBs, and one yard is one yard -- two leaves each ticking their own supplier-lead queue would land every order a batch early (`SITE_PHASES`). It also owns the coupled unit's THIRD OUTPUT, the contract's `site_inbound_db` (ADR-0005). A trailer's load is mixed by construction and a door is occupied by the trailer rather than by either channel's share of it, so those rows belong to neither leaf -- writing them to `leaves[0]` would be `a-right-site-total-hides-two-wrong-shares` built deliberately, which is exactly why site-dock 21 parked them and left the artifact to this ticket.
