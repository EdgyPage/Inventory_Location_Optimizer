def _take(self, leaf, what: str)
`leaf`'s share of `what` for this site day, served exactly once. The same "compute once, serve each caller once" contract `open_batch` keeps, and for a sharper reason: these are DRAINS. A leaf that asked twice would take the other channel's rows the second time only if the first call had left them — popping is what makes the second call raise instead.
