def daily_demand(orders, lines_per_day: float) -> dict
`{sku: d_s}` for one channel section at `lines_per_day` lines a day. The line share is `freq / Σ freq` over exactly the orders given -- the section the channel picks from (`staffing.regime_orders`), which is also how `expected_travel .accumulate` weights them, so the two agree by construction. An empty section or a zero weight sum yields zeros.
