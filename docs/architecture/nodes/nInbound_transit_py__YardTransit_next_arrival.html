def next_arrival(self)
`(arrival_s, trailer)` for the trailer in transit that arrives first, or None. The arrival instant is the event stamp `release` would give it (dispatch + lead). A trailer with no dispatch stamp or no lead never arrives mid-drain: the former has no calendar to arrive on, the latter arrived at `release` already.
