def noise_pmf(lot: float, cv: float) -> dict
The supply noise a fire lands with, epsilon = qty - ideal, as `{eps: prob}` over integers: `qty = max(1, round(Normal(ideal, ideal * cv)))` (`_fire_reorders`), taken at the typical lot. Zero noise (cv = 0) is `{0: 1}`.
