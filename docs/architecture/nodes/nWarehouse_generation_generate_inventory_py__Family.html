class Family
One category's SKU archetype. Every *_spec accepts any dist incl. 'mixture' (multimodal). length/width/height are sampled INDEPENDENTLY from their own specs; weight from weight_spec; demand from freq_spec (rate in (0,1]) and qty_spec (integer units/pick). handling_split is the per-family (conveyable, non-conveyable) propensity; share is the family's relative SKU fraction (normalized across the plan).
