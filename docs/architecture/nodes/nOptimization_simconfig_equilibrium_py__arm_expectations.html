def arm_expectations(expectations: dict, expected_pick: dict | None) -> dict
The expectations for ONE ARM: the pair's, with the picking band re-centred on the arm's own expected seconds per unit under its initial placement. The pair's pick expectation was drawn at the class-uniform `s_pick` (the demand's fixed point, so it equals rho by construction). An arm whose placement makes a unit cheaper is EXPECTED to run below it -- by the ratio of the two expectations, since the demand is shared -- and the band belongs around that number ("Derive the expected-travel closed form": the per-arm expectation is stamped for the report only). Without a stamped `expected_pick` (a flag-off arm, an older run) the pair's expectations are returned unchanged; the pick department is left alone when either `s_pick` is unknown.
