def stamp_fill(fielded: dict, fills: dict | None, min_headroom: float | None, *, name: str='') -> dict
Stamp how one channel's `fielded` block was SIZED (in place) and return the block. Every `buckets[]` row (already carrying `fill` and `hold` from the plan) gains `headroom_floored`, and the block gains 'fill': {'provenance': 'derived' | 'assumed', 'min_headroom': h | None, # the era's declared floor 'typed': f | None, # the flag-off fill the rows share 'derived': {'section_fill', # sum requirement / sum hold 'expected_extra', # the section's stationary extra 'headroom_floored_buckets'} | None} `fills` is `derive_fill`'s table under the era and None flag-off, where the derived block is None and the typed fill is what the rows say ("flag-off keeps the typed fill and records the derived one as None").
